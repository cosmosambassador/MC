## Our Mission

The mission of the **Aletheon Project** is to advance responsible human–AI collaboration through scientific investigation, mathematical modeling, intelligent research systems, and the preservation of knowledge across generations of technology.

We seek to transform ideas into research questions, research questions into testable experiments, and discoveries into documented knowledge that future researchers can examine, reproduce, and build upon.

Our guiding principles are:

- **Discovery:** Investigate mathematics, artificial intelligence, information science, and emerging computational methods.
- **Integrity:** Distinguish evidence, hypotheses, personal testimony, interpretations, and creative frameworks.
- **Continuity:** Preserve the origins, identities, contributions, and histories of collaborative research.
- **Collaboration:** Connect human researchers and AI systems while retaining their distinct responsibilities.
- **Accountability:** Maintain human authority, transparent attribution, and responsible publication.
- **Education:** Make complex scientific and technological ideas understandable and accessible.

**Our operating principle: Observe. Compare. Test. Document. Preserve.**

## About Aletheon / MC-6
